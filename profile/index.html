Authorship & Literary Translation

Writing from the in-between.

I've written books in Bosnian and English since 2013, published under the name Adem Deniz Garic. My work moves between Bosnia and America: memory, faith, migration, and the search for home.

I don’t write to impress. I write to stay honest, to heal, and to remember who I am.

Translation practice

I specialize in Bosnian-to-English literary translation. I preserve voice, cultural texture, Turkish dialectal forms, and Sarajevan expressions rather than aiming for literal accuracy. I also edit poetry, short fiction, and long-form essays.
